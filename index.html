Truth In Media

As Co-Founder of Universal Citizens Corp (UCMedia.Net), Lady Hamilton leads an international digital streaming platform broadcasting anytime, anyplace, anywhere in the world.

The network curates narratives that advance the liberation of individual mindsets beyond the constraints of mental incarceration—making a lasting, positive impact on people and the planet with purposes that benefit all communities equitably.

⚖

Global Voice & Advocacy

Operating from the Network's Protocol of Radiance, Lady Hamilton champions citizen journalists and media ambassadors who shine light on vital stories often overlooked by mainstream platforms.

Her work bridges continents, strengthens diasporic partnerships, and empowers local community leaders with modern distribution solutions.

Service & Designation

Leadership, Affiliations & Honors

01

President • The Gospel Institute

Preserving and advancing the monumental legacy of her late husband, the renowned Pastor, Professor, and musician Rev. Dr. Alexander Hamilton—celebrated for his orchestration and conducting of Aretha Franklin's historic Amazing Grace live concert album with Rev. James Cleveland. Lady Hamilton proudly reestablished the Gospel Institute and Gospel Music Institute in his honor.

02

First California Chair & Former Executive Board Member • ADDI

Serving the African Diaspora Development Institute under Her Excellency, Ambassador Arikana Chihombori-Quao, MD. Unifying the African Diaspora's collective global brain trust to foster self-sustainability and align with the African Union Agenda 2063. Co-created and launched the African Diaspora Congress (ADC) and served as Western Region Chair for AU PADWA.

03

Founder & President • PAC RED

Founded PAC RED (Preparation & Awareness for Community Resiliency in Emergencies & Disasters), training thousands of residents in underserved areas. Honored at the White House during the Obama Administration for Building Resilience with Diverse Communities by the DHS Center for Faith-Based and Neighborhood Partnerships. Served as a United Nations Global Compact Partner championing UN SDG Goal 16: Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions.

04

Public Safety & Community Resiliency Coalition Leader

Community Action Partner for DHS White House Center, California Volunteers EF-17, Los Angeles County Department of Public Health and Office of Emergency Management, American Red Cross West Coast Faith-Based Coordinator & Ambassador, Los Angeles Interfaith Clergy Coalition, and Faith Sector Leader for the LA Emergency Operations Center (BOC).
